二、服务内容

小学口算天天练为小学数学练习提供本地口算出题、题单预览、参考答案、竖式和脱式步骤、历史题单、常用模板、错题训练以及导出分享能力。

  • 按小学阶段预设或手动参数生成口算题单。
  • 生成整数、分数、整数和分数混合运算、竖式和脱式计算题。
  • 查看题单、参考答案和步骤，用于家长、老师或学生自查。
  • 在设备本地保存历史题单、常用模板、错题记录和设置。
  • 基于错题记录进行复练。
  • 主动分享文本或图片，或生成 Word 题单和 PDF 基础打印文件。

2. Service Content

Student Arithmetic Practice provides local arithmetic worksheet generation, worksheet preview, reference answers, vertical and step-by-step solutions, worksheet history, reusable templates, wrong-question practice, and export or sharing features for elementary math practice.

  • Generate arithmetic worksheets by elementary-grade presets or custom parameters.
  • Generate integer, fraction, mixed integer and fraction, vertical, and step-by-step calculation questions.
  • View worksheets, reference answers, and solution steps for parent, teacher, or student review.
  • Save worksheet history, templates, wrong-question records, and settings locally on the device.
  • Practice again based on wrong-question records.
  • Share text or images, or generate Word worksheets and basic PDF print files.
