五、儿童与监护人

本应用面向家长、老师和学生的口算练习场景。当前版本不要求儿童注册账号，也不主动上传儿童个人信息。家长或监护人可以通过应用内删除功能、系统清空应用数据或卸载应用来管理设备本地数据。

  • 导出：用户可主动将题单导出为文本、图片、Word 题单或 PDF 基础打印文件。

3. Permission Use

This version does not request special system permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, calendar, Bluetooth, or background running. When sharing text, images, Word worksheets, or basic PDF print files, the app calls the system share capability and lets the user choose the target. Canceling sharing does not affect worksheet generation, preview, saving, or wrong-question practice.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

This version does not integrate third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics, account services, payment services, or cloud sync, so it does not share personal information with third parties. If a later version adds third-party services or online capabilities, this policy will be updated before release and will describe the service name, purpose, and data scope in the app or app store listing.

5. Children and Guardians

The app is intended for arithmetic practice scenarios involving parents, teachers, and students. This version does not require children to register accounts and does not actively upload children's personal information. Parents or guardians may manage local device data by using in-app deletion, clearing app data in system settings, or uninstalling the app.

6. Access, Correction, Deletion, and Export

  • Access: users can view worksheet history, templates, wrong-question records, and settings in the app.
  • Correction: users can change settings, regenerate worksheets, save templates again, or delete and recreate content.
  • Deletion: users can delete worksheet history, templates, and wrong-question records, or clear app data through system settings or uninstall the app.
  • Export: users can actively export worksheets as text, images, Word worksheets, or basic PDF print files.

7. Policy Updates

If a later version adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, payment, third-party SDKs, online services, or new system permissions, this policy will be updated before release. The updated policy will be available on this page or through the in-app entry.

8. Contact Us

Users may contact us at tomkuku588@gmail.com, or submit feedback through app store comments or the feedback entry. For privacy-related issues, please do not submit sensitive personal information such as identity documents, precise addresses, or contact details in public comments.
